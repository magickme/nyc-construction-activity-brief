Suggested sort order

  1. Filter by work_type for the service line you care about.
  2. Filter by zip_code or borough for territory fit.
  3. Sort by issued_date to review the newest rows first.
  4. Use estimated_job_cost_bucket as a rough screen, not as a quote value.
  5. Open source_url before outreach, quoting, routing, or planning.
